# 1. Return Policy

## 1.1 Return Eligibility

Customers may request a return within **30 calendar days from the date the order was delivered**.

A product is eligible for return when:

- The return request is submitted within the 30-day return window.
- The product is unused or in resalable condition.
- The product includes its original packaging, accessories, manuals, and included components.
- The product is not listed as a non-returnable item.
- The product was purchased through SupportPilot's ecommerce platform.

The return eligibility period starts from the **actual delivery date**, not the order date.

## 1.2 Items That Cannot Normally Be Returned

The following items are generally not eligible for return:

- Personalized or customized products.
- Digital products that have already been downloaded or accessed.
- Gift cards.
- Products explicitly marked as "Final Sale".
- Products that have been intentionally damaged after delivery.
- Products missing essential components when the missing components are not due to an issue caused by SupportPilot.

## 1.3 Return Condition

Returned products should be:

- Unused.
- In their original packaging where applicable.
- Free from customer-caused damage.
- Returned with included accessories and components.

SupportPilot may reject a return when inspection determines that the product was damaged, modified, or substantially used by the customer.

## 1.4 Return Shipping Charges

Return shipping is free when:

- The wrong product was delivered.
- The product arrived damaged.
- The product arrived defective.
- SupportPilot made an error in fulfilling the order.

For customer-requested returns where the product is not defective or damaged, the customer may be responsible for return shipping costs.

## 1.5 Return Inspection

Returned products may be inspected after they reach the SupportPilot warehouse.

If the product passes inspection, the refund or exchange process will continue according to the applicable policy.

If the product fails inspection because of customer-caused damage or missing components, SupportPilot may reject the return or provide a partial refund.

# 2. Shipping Policy

## 2.1 Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within **1–2 business days** after successful payment confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may begin processing on the next business day.

## 2.2 Estimated Delivery

Estimated delivery dates are provided based on:

- Shipping destination.
- Selected shipping method.
- Product availability.
- Logistics partner capacity.

The estimated delivery date is not a guaranteed delivery date unless the order explicitly states that the delivery date is guaranteed.

## 2.3 Shipment Tracking

Customers can track shipped orders using the tracking information associated with their order.

The shipment status may include:

- Processing.
- Packed.
- Shipped.
- In Transit.
- At Sorting Facility.
- Out for Delivery.
- Delivered.
- Delivery Delayed.
- Delivery Exception.

## 2.4 Delayed Shipments

If an order has passed its estimated delivery date and has not been delivered, the customer may contact SupportPilot for assistance.

SupportPilot may investigate the shipment with the logistics provider.

A delayed shipment does not automatically qualify for a refund.

## 2.5 Lost Shipments

If a shipment is confirmed as lost by the logistics provider, SupportPilot may offer:

- A replacement, if the product is available.
- A full refund, if a replacement is unavailable or the customer chooses a refund where permitted.

A shipment is considered lost only after SupportPilot or its logistics provider confirms the loss.

## 2.6 Address Changes

Customers may request an address change before the order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, the delivery address generally cannot be changed through SupportPilot.

Customers may need to contact the logistics provider directly where an address modification option is available.

## 2.7 Shipping Damage

If a product arrives damaged, the customer should report the issue within **7 calendar days of delivery**.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs or other evidence of the damage.

Damaged products are handled under the Damaged Product Policy.

# 3. Refund Policy

## 3.1 Refund Eligibility

A customer may receive a refund when:

- A return is approved and the returned product passes inspection.
- The order is cancelled before shipment and payment has already been captured.
- A shipment is confirmed as lost and a replacement is not provided.
- A product is confirmed to be defective and a refund is approved.
- A damaged product is approved for refund instead of replacement.
- SupportPilot explicitly approves a refund as a customer-service resolution.

## 3.2 Refund Method

Refunds are normally issued to the **original payment method** used for the order.

SupportPilot does not normally transfer refunds to a different payment method.

## 3.3 Refund Processing Time

After a refund is approved, SupportPilot initiates the refund according to its internal processing procedure.

The time required for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment provider or bank.

SupportPilot cannot guarantee the exact time required by the customer's bank.

## 3.4 Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued when:

- The customer is permitted to keep a product with a minor issue.
- Some components of an order are missing.
- A returned product has a condition issue that does not justify a full refund.
- SupportPilot explicitly approves a partial refund.

The refund amount is determined based on the specific circumstances of the case.

## 3.5 Refund After Return

For standard returns, the refund is normally processed after the returned product is received and passes inspection.

Submitting a return request does not itself guarantee an immediate refund.

## 3.6 Shipping Charges

For returns caused by:

- Wrong product delivery.
- Damaged product.
- Defective product.
- Fulfillment errors.

SupportPilot will normally refund eligible shipping charges associated with the return.

For voluntary customer-requested returns, shipping charges may not be refundable.

# 4. Cancellation Policy

## 4.1 Cancellation Before Shipment

Customers may request cancellation while an order is still in the **Processing** stage.

If the cancellation is accepted before shipment, the order will be cancelled.

If payment has already been captured, an eligible refund will be initiated.

## 4.2 Cancellation After Shipment

Once an order has been shipped, it generally cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

The customer may instead:

1. Receive the order.
2. Request a return if the product is eligible.
3. Follow the applicable Return Policy.

## 4.3 Cancellation After Delivery

Delivered orders cannot be cancelled.

The customer must use the Return Policy if they want to send the product back.

## 4.4 Cancellation Due to Inventory Issues

SupportPilot may cancel an order when:

- The product becomes unavailable.
- Inventory information was incorrect.
- The order cannot be fulfilled.
- A technical or operational issue prevents fulfillment.

If SupportPilot cancels a paid order, the customer is eligible for a full refund of the amount paid for the cancelled order.

## 4.5 Duplicate Orders

If a customer accidentally places duplicate orders, they may request cancellation before shipment.

If one or more duplicate orders have already shipped, the customer must use the applicable return process.

---

# 5. Replacement Policy

## 5.1 Replacement Eligibility

A replacement may be offered when:

- The product arrived damaged.
- The product is defective.
- The wrong product was delivered.
- The shipment was confirmed lost.
- SupportPilot determines that replacement is an appropriate resolution.

## 5.2 Replacement Availability

Replacement is subject to product availability.

If the same product is unavailable, SupportPilot may offer:

- A refund.
- An alternative product of equivalent value, subject to customer approval.

## 5.3 Damaged Product Replacement

For products reported as damaged within **7 calendar days of delivery**, SupportPilot may provide a replacement after reviewing the customer's claim.

Photographs or other evidence may be requested.

The customer may be required to return the damaged product before the replacement is dispatched.

## 5.4 Defective Product Replacement

A product confirmed to be defective may qualify for replacement.

The defect must not have resulted from:

- Accidental damage.
- Improper use.
- Unauthorized modification.
- Normal wear and tear.

## 5.5 Wrong Product Replacement

If SupportPilot sends the wrong product, the customer may request a replacement with the correct product.

SupportPilot will normally cover the return shipping cost for the incorrectly delivered product.

## 5.6 Replacement Limits

SupportPilot may limit replacement requests when:

- The same product has already been replaced for the same issue.
- Evidence indicates customer-caused damage.
- The replacement request falls outside the applicable reporting period.
- The product is non-returnable or non-replaceable.

# 6. Damaged Product Policy

## 6.1 What Is Considered Damaged

A product is considered damaged when it arrives with physical damage that was not caused by the customer.

Examples include:

- Cracked product body.
- Broken component.
- Severe physical damage.
- Damaged packaging that has also damaged the product.
- Product received in unusable physical condition.

## 6.2 Reporting Period

Customers should report product damage within **7 calendar days from the delivery date**.

Claims reported after 7 days may require additional investigation and are not automatically approved.

## 6.3 Evidence Requirements

SupportPilot may request:

- Photos of the damaged product.
- Photos of the packaging.
- Photos of the shipping label.
- A description of the damage.
- A short video when necessary to demonstrate the issue.

Providing evidence does not automatically guarantee approval.

## 6.4 Available Resolutions

When a damage claim is approved, SupportPilot may provide:

1. Replacement of the same product.
2. Refund.
3. Partial refund where appropriate.

The available resolution depends on product availability and the circumstances of the case.

## 6.5 Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused after delivery by the customer is not covered by the Damaged Product Policy.

Examples include:

- Dropping the product.
- Water damage when the product is not water-resistant.
- Physical modification.
- Incorrect installation.
- Damage caused by misuse.

Such cases may be rejected.

## 6.6 Damaged Packaging but Undamaged Product

If only the external packaging is damaged but the product itself is unaffected and functional, the case does not automatically qualify for a replacement or full refund.

SupportPilot may review the case individually.

# 7. Policy Priority Rules

When multiple policies appear to apply, the following priority rules should be used.

## 7.1 Damaged Product

If a product arrived damaged, use the **Damaged Product Policy** first.

The general Return Policy should not override the specific damaged-product rules.

## 7.2 Defective Product

If the product is defective, use the **Replacement Policy** and applicable Refund Policy.

## 7.3 Wrong Product

If the wrong product was delivered, the customer is eligible for a supported resolution under the Replacement Policy.

Return shipping is normally free in this situation.

## 7.4 Lost Shipment

If the logistics provider confirms that the shipment is lost, use the Shipping Policy.

A replacement may be offered if inventory is available. Otherwise, a refund may be issued.

## 7.5 Cancellation

If the order has not been shipped, use the Cancellation Policy.

If the order has already shipped, cancellation is generally unavailable and the customer should use the Return Policy where applicable.

# 8. Important Policy Definitions

## Delivery Date

The date on which the shipment is recorded as delivered by the logistics provider.

## Shipment Date

The date on which the order is handed over to the logistics provider.

## Processing

The order has been accepted but has not yet been shipped.

## Shipped

The order has been handed over to the logistics provider.

## Damaged Product

A product that arrives with physical damage that was not caused by the customer.

## Defective Product

A product that does not function according to its intended purpose due to a manufacturing or product fault.

## Business Day

A normal working day excluding weekends and applicable public holidays.
